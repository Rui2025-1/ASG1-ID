About Divergent Divers

Founded in 2010, we share a mission: sharing the magic of the underwater world while upholding the highest standard of safety and conservation.

Our track record

Divergent Divers has established itself as a premier diving center recognized from excellence in diver education and marine conservation. Over the past decade, we have certified thousands of divers, from beginner divers to our deep divers. Our squad of multilingual instructors have decades of experience from oceans around the world.
